Triage that keeps working when the signal doesn't.

RuralCare is an offline-first intake and triage app for frontline health workers. Register patients, catch danger signs and prepare referrals with no connection. Every record syncs on its own when the network comes back.

Log in to RuralCare

On iPhone, tap the Share icon in Safari, then choose Add to Home Screen.

Works in any modern browser. Install it once and it opens like a normal app, with or without data.

Designed around the clinic, not the cloud.

Each part of RuralCare assumes the network will fail at the worst moment, and keeps going anyway.

Records that never wait for a signal

Every patient is saved on the device first and queued. A dropped connection mid-visit loses nothing.

Flags that account for age

Pulse, blood pressure, fever and breathing rate are checked against ranges for infants, children and adults, with extra rules for pregnancy.

A clinical assistant on the phone

Ask for the urgent queue, type vitals for an instant triage, or open the fever, blood pressure, oxygen and dehydration protocols. It runs on the device.

Clinical notes by voice

Speak and your words appear as text in the notes. An offline mode takes over when there is no connection.

Referral notes from the record

Build a referral note in one tap, with vitals and findings filled in and space left for the treatment given.

Sync that retries on its own

Queued records upload as soon as a working connection returns, and try again if the link is weak.

A visit, step by step.

  1. 1

    Register

    Enter the patient, their vitals and symptoms. Dictate the notes if your hands are busy.

  2. 2

    Flag

    The queue sorts itself into urgent, review and clear, using ranges for the patient's age.

  3. 3

    Refer

    Open the checklist and prepare a referral note. If there is no data, send a short summary by SMS.

  4. 4

    Sync

    When a connection returns, queued records upload without anyone pressing a button.

Patient data stays on the device until you sync it.

The triage rules, the clinical assistant and offline voice notes all run in the browser on the phone. No outside AI service receives your patient records.

Live dictation uses your browser's own speech service when you are online. Offline dictation keeps the audio on the device.

RuralCare supports clinical judgement and does not replace it. Triage guidance is version 1.0 and is pending clinical review.
